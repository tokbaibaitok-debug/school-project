ABOUT CYBERSECURITY

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity protects digital systems and information from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft. It is important for individuals, schools, businesses, and organizations.

🔐

Data Protection

Keep personal and sensitive information safe from unauthorized access.

🖥️

Device Security

Protect computers, phones, and other devices from malicious software.

🌐

Network Security

Secure networks against unauthorized access and suspicious activity.

COMMON THREATS

Know the Risks

01

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick people into revealing information.

02

Malware

Malicious software that can harm devices or compromise information.

03

Ransomware

Malicious software that can prevent access to files or systems.

04

Password Attacks

Attempts to gain unauthorized access to accounts using stolen or guessed passwords.

STAY PROTECTED

Cybersecurity Safety Tips

01

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts.

02

Enable MFA

Use multi-factor authentication whenever available.

03

Update Software

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

04

Think Before Clicking

Be careful with unexpected links and attachments.

05

Back Up Your Data

Keep backups of important files in a safe location.

06

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive information with unknown people or websites.
